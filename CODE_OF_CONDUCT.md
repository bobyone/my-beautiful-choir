# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation
in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility for our mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions) and when an individual officially represents the
project.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[bobyone](https://github.com/bobyone), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/bobyone/my-beautiful-choir/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of conduct". All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and
the privacy of the reporter is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
